MARKPAD - DEVELOPMENT AND RELEASES

Development project: C:\Repos\markpad
Open Markpad.lnk to run the development app in bin\Markpad.exe.
Keep the Electron support files together in bin.

Build-Releases.cmd creates the Windows installer and portable ZIP in
dist\releases. Publish-Markpad.cmd pushes committed source to GitHub.
After reviewing the build, use Publish-Markpad.cmd -Release to publish
the installer and portable ZIP to kingjoyeuse/markpad.

See README.md and docs\RELEASING.md for build requirements and instructions.
